## 2. What Instagram rewards in 2026 — and why it changes the plan

Researched today, and it contradicts the existing content plan in one important
way.

| Signal | What the evidence says | Source label |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels vs carousels for reach** | Reels average roughly **3× the reach** of static posts and carousels (~182 vs ~59 in one widely-cited 2026 dataset) | **INFERRED** — third-party aggregate, not our data |
| **Shares, especially DM sends** | The strongest ranking input. DM shares reported as weighted **3–5× higher than likes** for Reels distribution, and shares from non-followers weigh heaviest of all | **INFERRED** |
| **Small accounts** | Accounts under 10k reportedly grow fastest by treating **Reels as the entire strategy** for a quarter, layering carousels once an audience exists | **INFERRED** |
| **Suggested mix** | 3–4 Reels/week, 2–3 carousels, 1–2 static | **INFERRED** |

**Nothing here is MEASURED for TRENIKO.** The account has 2 followers and no
meaningful reach data of its own. Treat all of it as a prior to be overwritten
by Instagram Insights once anything publishes.

### The consequence

**Cycles 1 and 2 are carousel-heavy.** For an account with 2 followers and 0%
non-follower reach, that is backwards: carousels convert an audience you already
have, and TRENIKO does not have one. **Reels are the only format that reaches
someone who does not already follow you.**

**Do not rewrite the calendar.** Do reweight what gets published first: publish
the Reels, hold the carousels for when there is somebody to convert.

### The design principle that matters most

**Make it sendable.** If DM shares are the heaviest signal, the question for
every piece is not "will this get likes" but **"would one trainer forward this
to another trainer?"**

That is a very different bar, and it happens to favour exactly the content
TRENIKO already has: *"How many sessions has she got left? Ten seconds. Go."* is
sendable. *"5 tips for personal trainers"* is not.

## 5. Ten Reel concepts — the priority format

Each is ≤20 seconds, needs no face, and passes the sendability test.

1. **The countdown.** Type `10` bought, `6` used. The remaining cell fills in `4`
   by itself. Change 6→7, remaining becomes 3. *"The one column you should never
   type."*
2. **Three places, one question.** Phone → calendar → spreadsheet → each shows a
   different number. *"Which one is right?"*
3. **The 21:40 message.** A phone at night: *"Hey, how many sessions do I have
   left?"* Cut to a spreadsheet being opened in bed.
4. **Two minutes, one client.** Real-time: register, add a client, book a
   session. No cuts. *"That's the whole trial."*
5. **The renewal you missed.** Package hits zero mid-session. *"You find out
   during the last session. That's the expensive version."*
6. **Cancelled ≠ no-show.** Two cards side by side, different consequences.
7. **What an hour really pays.** The calculator: 40 for a 60-minute session, 100
   minutes of actual work, → 24/hour.
8. **The discount that bought nothing.** 10 sessions, 10% off = 40 given away.
   *"What did that buy you?"*
9. **The weekly ten minutes.** Three questions: who has ≤2 left, who owes money,
   who has not trained in 3 weeks.
10. **What TRENIKO does not do.** *"No programming. No nutrition. No online
    coaching."* Honest, and it filters.

## 8. Interaction — the part that is actually missing

The account has never interacted with anyone. This matters more than the next
ten posts.

**Daily, ~15 minutes:**

- **Follow 3–5 Croatian trainers** who match the profile — a booking link in
  bio, prices or packages mentioned, several different clients in posts. Build
  to ~100 over a month. **Not 500 in a day.**
- **Leave 3–5 genuine comments.** Not "🔥" — something that shows you read it. A
  trainer posting about a client's progress does not need your emoji.
- **Reply to every comment and DM within a day.**

**Do not:** mass-follow, follow-unfollow, mass-like, automate anything, buy
anything, or join pods. All are against the constraints and most are against
Instagram's terms.

**Why this precedes any DM:** a cold DM from an account with 2 followers that
follows nobody is spam. The same DM from an account that has been following and
genuinely engaging for three weeks is a conversation. That is the entire
difference, and it costs nothing but time.

## 10. Turning a profile visit into a registration

The path, and where it currently breaks:

```
Reel (reach) → profile visit → bio explains it in 3 seconds → link
→ free tracker → download → the page explains where a sheet stops
→ /register (free, no card) → add one client
```

**Measurable today:** everything from the link onwards — tracker page views,
downloads, registration, verification, first client — split by
`utm_content=link-in-bio` in the admin funnel.

**Not measurable:** impressions, profile visits, and link taps. Those live in
Instagram Insights and cannot be joined to our data. **Read them separately and
do not pretend the two are one funnel.**

**The honest expectation:** at 2 followers this produces nothing for weeks.
Instagram is a compounding channel and it is not how the first ten trainers
arrive. It ranks below messaging trainers you know, local gyms, and the
education providers — and it must not be allowed to displace them, because it
feels like work while producing nothing.

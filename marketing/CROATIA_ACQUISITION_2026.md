# TRENIKO — Croatian distribution, researched

**Researched:** 25 Aug 2026 · **€0** · Public sources only. Nothing submitted,
no accounts created, no messages sent.

The honest framing first: TRENIKO's one structural advantage over Trainerize,
TrueCoach, PTminder, FitSW and Everfit is not a feature. It is that a founder in
Croatia can reach Croatian trainers personally, in their language, in a market
none of those five is working. This file is about spending that advantage.

**Rule for every row:** "free" and "allowed" mean I checked, or the row says I
could not.

## 1. The single best lead source found

### fitness-treneri.hr — a public directory of Croatian personal trainers

<https://www.fitness-treneri.hr/index.php/osobni-treneri/>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Personal trainers in Croatia, listed by themselves |
| **Size** | ~16 pages of listings, ~150+ trainers |
| **Language** | Croatian |
| **Browsing** | **Free**, no account, no paywall observed |
| **Public per listing** | Name, photo, city, specialisations, first-person bio |
| **Contact** | On the individual profile, not the index |
| **Are they the ICP?** | **Largely yes.** The site has a "Za trenere" join section and the bios are written in the first person — these are trainers marketing *themselves*, which is the defining trait of the target |

**Why this matters more than any directory TRENIKO could be listed in:** it is a
list of people who, by the act of listing themselves, have told you they find
their own clients. A trainer who finds their own clients books their own
sessions and takes their own money — which is the entire precondition for the
package-tracking problem to exist.

**Approach — and the line not to cross.** This is a source for *identifying* the
profile, not a contact list to work through. Read a profile, and if the person
genuinely matches, reach them the way any prospective client would: through the
contact they published. **Ten personalised messages over a week, not 150 over an
afternoon.** A trainer who realises they were row 87 of a scrape will say so
publicly, and in a market this small that is unrecoverable.

**Can TRENIKO be listed here?** No — it lists trainers, not software. Its value
is entirely as a lead source.

## 2. Croatian trainer education — the strongest partnership angle

Their students are newly independent trainers who are about to need exactly this,
and have no incumbent tool to switch away from.

| Provider | URL | What it is | Why it fits | Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HFS Academy** | `hfsacademy.hr` | Croatian Fitness Association academy; EREPS-accredited certification | Graduates are newly certified trainers starting out | Email offering the free tracker + calculator as a student resource. **No payment, no affiliate, no commission** — a genuine free resource or nothing |
| **Fitnes učilište** | `fitnes-uciliste.hr` | Personal trainer education | Same | Same |
| **Flexyfit Academy** | `flexyfitacademy.com/hr` | Fitness/PT courses, online and in person | Same | Same |
| **Hrvatski kineziološki savez** | — | Issues the licence required to work legally | Institutional, slow, unlikely to link to a product | Low priority. Do not chase |

**Why this beats a directory listing:** a resource recommended by the body that
certified you carries trust a listing never will. And it is free on both sides —
the tracker and the calculator genuinely help a new trainer whether or not they
ever use TRENIKO.

**What not to do:** do not ask for a backlink, do not offer revenue share, do not
send a sales deck. Offer the two free resources, say what they are, and let them
decide. If they say no, that is the end of it.

`FIRST_10_USERS_EXECUTION_2026.md` § 4. Not sent, and not sendable by me.

## 3. Facebook groups — the highest-value channel I cannot assess

Croatian fitness and trainer groups are where the ICP concentrates, and **their
rules are not readable from outside**: the groups are private, and membership is
the only way to see the pinned rules.

That is not a gap I can research around, and it is the reason this row keeps
saying "needs you".

**The approach that works, and the one that ends it:**

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Join. Read the pinned rules **first** | Post anything in week one |
| Answer questions for two weeks with no link and no mention | Drop the link and leave |
| Mention TRENIKO only if someone asks what you use, and only if the rules permit | Mention it in a thread you started |
| Accept a "no promotion" rule as permanent | Wait until "nobody is looking" |

**Search terms to find them:** `personalni treneri Hrvatska`, `fitness treneri
Hrvatska`, `osobni trener Zagreb`, `fitness zajednica Hrvatska`.

**Realistic expectation:** most will ban promotion outright. The value is not the
link — it is that you will learn how trainers actually describe this problem in
Croatian, which is worth more than a post.

## 5. Recognising the target from public information

The profile: **an independent trainer who books their own clients and sells
blocks of sessions.**

From a public Instagram or directory profile, the signals that it is them:

| Signal | Why it indicates the ICP |
|---|---|
| **A booking link in the bio** — Calendly, a form, "DM za termin" | They handle their own scheduling. Nobody else does it for them |
| **Prices or packages mentioned** — "10 treninga", "paket od 10" | **The strongest single signal.** The package-tracking problem cannot exist without packages |
| **"DM za informacije" / "javi se na DM"** | Bookings arrive as messages, which is where the tracking problem starts |
| **Client transformation posts, several different clients** | An active book of clients, not one or two |
| **Own brand rather than a gym's** | Independent, not an employee |
| **Posts about their own schedule** — "ostalo mi je 2 termina ovaj tjedan" | They are managing capacity by hand |

Signals it is **not** them, and to move on:

- Online-only coaching / programme sales — TRENIKO does not do programme delivery
- A gym's account rather than a person's — the receptionist books
- Nutrition or influencer content with no visible client work
- Fewer than ~5 clients evident — a notebook genuinely wins and they are right

**Collect nothing.** Read the profile, decide, message or move on. Do not build a
list of people you have not contacted, do not export anything, and do not record
anything about someone who never replied beyond "no reply".

## 6. What this changes about the plan

1. **There is a real, ethical lead source** — ~150 self-identified independent
   Croatian trainers with public profiles. That removes "I don't know who to
   message" as an obstacle.
2. **The education providers are the best non-personal channel available**, and
   are three emails, not a campaign.
3. **Facebook groups stay unassessable from here.** They are likely the highest
   value and they need you to join before anything can be said about them.
4. **No Croatian directory is worth chasing for acquisition.** Trainers are not
   in startup directories. Any value there is entity signal, and it is small.

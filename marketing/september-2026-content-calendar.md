## Cadence

Instagram feed lands **three times a week**: Tue/Thu/Sat in weeks 3 and 4,
Mon/Wed on the closing week. Stories run **two to three times a week**, never on
a day with no feed post to hang off except the pure-research ones.

Facebook takes the **same eight posts with rewritten copy**, timed 30–60 minutes
apart from Instagram so the two do not publish in the same minute.

## Posting times — hypotheses, not findings

| Slot | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| **11:00–11:30** | The gap between the early-morning block and midday clients. |
| **12:00–12:30** | Lunch, the longest reliable break in a trainer's day. |
| **18:00–19:00** | Between the last two evening sessions. |
| **19:00–20:00** | After the last client, which is when admin actually gets done. |

These are **starting guesses, deliberately varied so the month teaches us
something**. Nobody has measured TRENIKO's audience by hour. Read per-post reach
in Instagram Insights at the end of the month and reset the grid from data, not
from this table.

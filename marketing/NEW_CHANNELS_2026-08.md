## 1. Facebook **Page-to-Page** messaging — the one that fits your constraint exactly

**This is the find.** Facebook has a Page-to-Page messaging feature: the TRENIKO
Page can message another business Page directly. **No personal profile is
involved at any point.**

That matters because it routes around the wall every previous plan hit — Groups
need your profile, DMs need your profile, but Page → Page does not.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | €0 |
| **Personal profile needed?** | **No** — this is the point |
| **Who to message** | Croatian personal trainers who run a business Page, and small gyms/studios in Varaždin and Zagreb |
| **Limit** | Only Pages with messaging enabled can receive one. If there is no *Send message* button, that Page has it off — move on |
| **Risk** | Low, if personalised. High if it becomes volume — Meta treats repeated identical Page messages as spam |

**How to turn it on:** Page → **Settings → Privacy → Messages** (Facebook's help
article is "Enable Page-to-Page Messaging"). Verify the Page can both send and
receive before sending the first one.

### The message — Croatian, from the Page

> Pozdrav! Javljam se iz Trenika — radimo jednostavan alat za osobne trenere za
> vođenje klijenata, paketa i termina.
>
> Ne prodajemo ništa i nemamo pretplatu. Tražimo nekoliko trenera koji bi ga
> probali i rekli nam što im fali i što nema smisla.
>
> Ako vas zanima, tu je i besplatna tablica za praćenje klijenata i paketa —
> bez registracije, bez ostavljanja maila:
> treniko.com/free-personal-trainer-client-tracker
>
> Ako nije za vas, skroz u redu i hvala na vremenu.

**Rules for yourself, and they are the whole thing:**

- **Five a day, maximum.** This is a conversation channel, not a broadcast.
- **Personalise the first line every time.** If you cannot name something
  specific about that trainer, do not message them.
- **Never the same text twice in the same week.**
- **One message. No follow-up** unless they reply.

## 2. Direct email to gyms — Varaždin and Zagreb

Gyms publish business email addresses precisely so businesses can contact them.
A personalised B2B email to a published business address is not spam; a hundred
identical ones are.

**Who to write to:** small independent gyms and studios — the ones where the
trainers on the floor find and bill their own clients. **Not** chains: their
trainers are employees and the admin problem is not theirs.

**The angle is not "please use our software".** It is "do your trainers handle
their own bookings — and if so, could I show two of them something free?"

### The email

> **Predmet:** Pitanje o trenerima kod vas (i jedna besplatna stvar za njih)
>
> Poštovani,
>
> Zovem se [ime] i radim TRENIKO — jednostavan alat za osobne trenere za
> vođenje klijenata, paketa i odrađenih termina.
>
> Ne pišem da bih nešto prodavao. Zanima me jedna stvar: dogovaraju li treneri
> kod vas sami svoje termine i naplatu, ili to ide preko recepcije?
>
> Ako se sami snalaze, imamo besplatnu tablicu za praćenje klijenata i paketa
> koju slobodno mogu koristiti — bez registracije i bez naplate:
> treniko.com/free-personal-trainer-client-tracker
>
> Ako bi nekome od njih bilo zanimljivo probati i sam alat, rado bih čuo što im
> fali. Ako ne, hvala na vremenu i ispričavam se na smetnji.
>
> Lijep pozdrav,
> [ime] · [telefon] · treniko.com

**Ten a week, each one actually read before sending.** The question at the
centre — *do your trainers book their own clients* — is the qualifying question
for the whole business, so even a "no, reception does it" reply is useful.

## 3. Netokracija — free editorial coverage, Croatia-wide

<https://www.netokracija.com> — the main Croatian tech and startup outlet.

Their contact page invites *"Imate zanimljivu priču"* (do you have an
interesting story) and separately mentions *"želite oglašavati"* (want to
advertise). **Editorial is the free path; advertising is the paid one. Pitch the
story, never the ad.**

**Pitch to:** `info@netokracija.com`

**Do not pitch "new app launches".** That is not a story. The story that is
actually true and interesting:

### The pitch

> **Predmet:** Hrvatski alat za osobne trenere — i zašto smo prvo napravili
> besplatnu tablicu
>
> Poštovani,
>
> Radim TRENIKO, hrvatski alat za osobne trenere: klijenti, paketi i koliko je
> kojem klijentu ostalo treninga.
>
> Ne javljam se s objavom o lansiranju. Mislim da je zanimljivija priča ovo:
> prije samog alata napravili smo besplatnu tablicu i besplatni kalkulator
> cijena, bez registracije i bez prikupljanja mailova — jer većina trenera taj
> problem danas rješava u tri odvojena alata i ne treba im četvrti dok ne vide
> vrijedi li.
>
> Zanimljiv kut za tekst: koliko administracije zapravo nosi rad samostalnog
> trenera u Hrvatskoj, i zašto skoro svi vode pakete ručno.
>
> Rado ću poslati brojke, screenshotove ili detalje ako vas zanima. Nemamo
> korisničke brojke za hvaliti — projekt je u ranoj fazi i to je dio priče.
>
> Lijep pozdrav,
> [ime] · treniko.com

**Why this shape:** it offers a topic rather than an announcement, it is honest
about being early, and it gives them something to write *about* instead of
something to republish. That is the difference between a pitch that lands and
one that is deleted.

## 4. Fitness.com.hr — where Croatian trainers actually read

<https://www.fitness.com.hr> — the largest Croatian fitness portal. They publish
trainer interviews and round-ups (e.g. a feature on the most influential
Croatian trainers).

**Two uses, and the second is better:**

1. **Editorial pitch** — an article on the business side of training: tracking
   packages, cancellation policies, pricing. Written for trainers, with no
   product pitch in it. Same approach as § 3.
2. **A lead source.** Their trainer interviews and round-ups are a public,
   curated list of **influential Croatian trainers who already talk publicly
   about their work**. That is a better-qualified list than any directory,
   because someone who gives interviews will usually answer a message.

## 6. Show HN and Dev.to — for a link and credibility, not customers

Be clear-eyed: **these produce founders and developers, not trainers.**

| Channel | Cost | Account | Worth it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hacker News — Show HN** | €0 | Needs an account (**yours to create**) | Real traffic spike, a strong backlink, honest feedback |
| **Dev.to** | €0 | Needs an account | A durable technical post and a backlink |

**Post the RLS write-up, not the product.** The story — *"our activation metric
read zero for months; it was row-level security, not the product"* — is
genuinely novel, useful to anyone running Postgres with RLS, and true. TRENIKO
is incidental context. Full draft in `REDDIT_DISTRIBUTION_2026.md` § 4C.

A "Show HN: software for personal trainers" post with no users will be ignored.
The RLS post will not.

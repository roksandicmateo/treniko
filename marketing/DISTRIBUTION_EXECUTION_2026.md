## 1. A correction to earlier research

`RESEARCH_2026.md` § 2 called the free-template market for personal trainers
"thin", on the basis of a search-results scan where the free results were mostly
workout templates and the client trackers were mostly paid Etsy listings.

**That was wrong, and the correction matters.** TrainerStudio runs
[a library of 30+ free Excel and PDF templates](https://www.trainerstudio.com/en/templates)
— client tracking, client onboarding, contracts, invoices, progress trackers —
used for exactly the purpose TRENIKO uses its tracker for, ending in "Try
TrainerStudio free".

Two consequences:

1. **The strategy is validated.** A funded competitor invests in this because it
   works. Free templates → trainer → product is a real path, not a hopeful one.
2. **The position must be narrower.** Out-publishing a 30-template library is not
   available. What is available is being *better at one thing*: TRENIKO's tracker
   has a working countdown formula and ships as a real `.xlsx` that opens on a
   European locale, and the pricing calculator is interactive. A static template
   library has neither. **TrainerStudio has no pricing calculator** — checked.

This is why the calculator was built today rather than a fourth template.

## 4. Backlinks — and the finding that closes most of this off

The standard tactic in this niche is "find the *best free tools for personal
trainers* roundups and ask to be added". **It mostly does not work here**, and it
is worth knowing why before spending a week on it.

Who actually publishes those articles:

| Publisher | What they are | Will they list TRENIKO? |
|---|---|---|
| FitBudd, WAGMI, KeegNation, Trainerize | **Competitors** | No |
| PTPioneer, TrainerAcademy | **Affiliate-monetised** | Only for commission — that is a paid placement, which is out of scope |
| Guideflow | SaaS demo vendor | Marginal |
| TrainerStudio | **Competitor** with the template library | No |

So the roundup route is largely competitor-owned or pay-to-play. **Do not budget
effort against it.** What remains genuinely open:

| Opportunity | Why TRENIKO fits | What to link | Ask or contribute? | Needs you? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The free calculator** | Calculators get linked where articles do not, and it suggests no prices — so it is citable by people who would not link a vendor page | `/personal-trainer-pricing-calculator` | Neither — it earns links by existing and being found | Only to share it |
| **thePTDC** | Publishes trainer business content, accepts contributions | An article, not a link | **Contribute.** Pitch the cancellation-policy material — a real disagreement among trainers, argued without selling anything | ✅ Pitch |
| **Technical write-up** (prerender / cookieless analytics) | Genuinely novel and reusable; TRENIKO is incidental | Nothing, or the repo | Contribute | ✅ Post |
| **Croatian fitness education providers** | Their graduates are the ICP at the exact moment they start needing this | Tracker + calculator | Contribute, as a resource for students | ✅ Email |
| **Small trainer-educators** (<10k) | Reachable, and a free tool is giftable | Tracker + calculator | Gift, with no ask | ✅ Message |

**Never:** purchased links · PBNs · link farms · automated submission · comment
spam · fake profiles · guest posts written only for the link.

**Honest expectation:** TRENIKO will not build meaningful link authority in six
months and nothing free changes that. The tracker and the calculator are the only
two assets with a real chance of being linked, because they are useful without
TRENIKO.

# TRENIKO — Instagram Stories, 15–30 September 2026

Ten sequences, 2–3 frames each, all 1080 × 1920, all English.
Assets: `marketing/september-2026/stories/<slug>/frame-N.png`.

**Read this before publishing.** Where a sequence uses a **poll**, a **question
box** or an **emoji slider**, the rendered frame carries the question as type and
leaves the sticker's space empty. The sticker is added in the Instagram mobile
app at publish time. A drawn poll cannot be voted on, and the answers are the
entire reason those Stories exist.

Meta Business Suite **can** schedule Stories, but its composer offers only *Edit*
and *Add link* per card — **no poll and no question sticker**. So the plain and
link-only frames can be scheduled; the sticker ones are published by hand. This
is marked per sequence below and repeated in `../scheduling/README.md`.

Link stickers use:
`https://treniko.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=sep2026&utm_content=<story-id>`

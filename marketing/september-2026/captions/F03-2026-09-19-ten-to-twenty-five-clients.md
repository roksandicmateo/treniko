## CAPTION — Instagram

> At ten clients you can hold the whole business in your head. Who is booked,
> who has sessions left, who still owes for the last pack.
>
> Somewhere in the twenties that stops working, and it does not announce itself.
> You just start double-checking things. Then you start checking the bank app.
> Then a client you have not seen for three weeks turns out to have quietly
> stopped booking.
>
> Nothing broke. The system you built for ten people is being asked to carry
> twenty-five.

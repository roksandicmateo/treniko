## 2. Product Hunt — verified, and still wait

**It is free.** No listing fee, no hunter fee; maker-led launches perform as well
as hunted ones.

**Requirements, as they stand in 2026:**

- A maker account, and it should be **aged ~30+ days** before launching
- An **Upcoming page** so an audience is waiting on the day
- Launch at **00:01 Pacific**, mid-week
- ~18 hours of replying to every comment on the day

**Recommendation: do not launch. One thing to do now anyway.**

| Condition | Now |
|---|---|
| Real users who would comment | **0 acquired trainers** |
| Anything true to say about traction | Nothing |
| Launch-day audience | 2 Instagram followers, 1 Page follower |
| Screenshots | Not yet |

A launch is one-shot. Spending it with nobody to comment converts the largest
free attention event available into a handful of founder upvotes.

**Do now (free, and it expires nothing):** create the maker account, because the
30-day ageing requirement means the account must exist well before the decision
to launch. Do not create the Upcoming page yet.

**Launch trigger — all four:** ~10 active trainers, several of whom would
genuinely comment · screenshots exist · one true sentence about traction that is
not "we just launched" · an Upcoming page live for 2+ weeks.

**And a caveat worth stating:** Product Hunt's audience is founders and
early-adopter tech people. It is good for a backlink and a traffic spike. It is
**not** where Croatian personal trainers are, and it should never be mistaken for
the thing that gets you trainers.

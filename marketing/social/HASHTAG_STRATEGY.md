# TRENIKO — hashtag strategy

**Written:** 18 Aug 2026, session 6. All tags **English**, matching the
account's single-language rule in `STRATEGY.md`.

---

## The principle

A hashtag is a routing instruction, not decoration. The only question that
matters is: **would a personal trainer running their own business browse this
tag?** `#fitness` has hundreds of millions of posts and is browsed by people
looking for workouts — TRENIKO's buyer is not in there. `#ptbusiness` is far
smaller and is browsed by exactly the right person.

So the tags below are chosen for **audience precision, not volume**. A small tag
that reaches 200 of the right people beats a huge one that reaches 50,000 of the
wrong ones, and it is the only kind of reach an account with 2 followers can
realistically win.

**Sizes are unverified.** Nobody has read live post counts for these tags. The
size column is a rough intent band — treat it as a plan, not a measurement, and
correct it once `30-day/METRICS_TEMPLATE.md` has real per-post reach data.

## The clusters

### C1 — PERSONAL TRAINER (identity)
Who they are. Broadest of the six, used sparingly.

`#personaltrainer` · `#personaltrainers` · `#ptlife` · `#strengthcoach`
· `#onlinecoach` · `#onlinepersonaltrainer`

### C2 — COACHING BUSINESS (the buyer's job)
The single most on-target cluster. Someone browsing here is thinking about the
business, not the workout.

`#coachingbusiness` · `#ptbusiness` · `#personaltrainingbusiness`
· `#fitnessbusiness` · `#coachingtools` · `#trainerbusiness`

### C3 — FITNESS BUSINESS (industry)
Adjacent operators — gym owners, studios, small teams.

`#fitnessbusiness` · `#gymowner` · `#studioowner` · `#fitpro` · `#fitpros`
· `#fitnessindustry`

### C4 — TRAINER PRODUCTIVITY (the pain)
Pairs with Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 posts. Small tags, high intent.

`#lessadmin` · `#adminwork` · `#coachingadmin` · `#timemanagement`
· `#workflow` · `#getorganised`

### C5 — CLIENT MANAGEMENT (the job to be done)
Pairs with product-led posts.

`#clientmanagement` · `#clienttracking` · `#sessiontracking`
· `#clientprogress` · `#coachingsoftware` · `#trainingsoftware`

### C6 — FITNESS ENTREPRENEUR (aspiration)
Weakest of the six — high noise, plenty of drop-shipping and hustle content.
Use one tag from it at most, and only on business-growth posts.

`#fitnessentrepreneur` · `#solopreneur` · `#smallbusinessowner`
· `#buildyourbusiness`

## How many, and which

**5 to 8 per feed post. 2 to 4 per Reel.**

Not 30. Instagram's own guidance is 3–5, hashtag stuffing is on the *Never*
list in `STRATEGY.md`, and a wall of tags under a post from a 2-follower account
reads as desperate — which is a brand cost that no amount of reach repays.

The five existing Reels carry exactly two (`#personaltrainer #ptbusiness`).
**Leave them.** They are already written into the caption files and the point of
this document is the next cycle, not a rewrite of the last one.

| Pillar | Recipe | Example set |
|---|---|---|
| **Trainer pain** | C4 ×2–3 + C2 ×2 + C1 ×1 | `#coachingadmin #lessadmin #ptbusiness #coachingbusiness #personaltrainer` |

| **Relatable** | C1 ×2 + C4 ×1 + C2 ×1 | `#personaltrainer #ptlife #coachingadmin #ptbusiness` |

### Placement

**First comment, not the caption**, for feed posts. The caption is the thing a
person reads; the tags are the thing the algorithm reads. Keeping them apart
means the caption's last line can be the CTA instead of a wall of blue. Reels
put their two tags inline — at that count it costs nothing.

---

## Rotate, don't repeat

**Never post the identical tag block twice in a row.** Repetition looks
automated to Instagram and teaches you nothing: if every post carries the same
six tags, no post can ever tell you which tags work.

Vary at least two tags between consecutive posts, and keep one constant anchor
(`#ptbusiness`) so there is a baseline to compare against.

---

## Never

- More than 8 tags on a feed post, or 4 on a Reel
- `#fitness`, `#gym`, `#workout`, `#fitfam`, `#motivation` — enormous, and
  browsed by people who want to train, not people who want to run a training
  business
- `#followforfollow`, `#likeforlike`, `#f4f`, `#explorepage`, `#viral` — these
  attract bots, and a follower who is a bot is worse than no follower because it
  poisons your reach ratio permanently
- Banned or shadowbanned tags. Before adding a tag that is new to this list,
  search it in the app and check that a **Recent** section actually appears —
  if a tag shows only Top posts, it is restricted
- Tags in any language other than English — same rule as all public copy
- Location tags for a place TRENIKO does not occupy

## Prove it or drop it

This whole document is a hypothesis. Nothing here has been measured, because the
account has produced no per-post reach data yet.

From cycle 2 on, record in `30-day/METRICS_TEMPLATE.md` for every post: the tag
set used, and the reach split between followers and non-followers. After ten
posts, drop the clusters that never appear in the non-follower half.

**C6 is the first candidate for deletion.** It is included for completeness and
is the least likely to survive contact with data.

## Why the Page exists

Be honest about this, because it changes what belongs on it.

The Page was **not** created because personal trainers are waiting on Facebook
for TRENIKO. It was created because **Meta Business Suite reaches Instagram only
through a linked Facebook Page**. No Page, no Instagram scheduling — which is
why five Reels have been stuck in manual phone-publishing for five sessions.

The Page is infrastructure first and an audience second. That ordering should
drive every decision below, and it means the correct amount of effort to spend
on Facebook-native content right now is **small**.

Two secondary benefits, both real but minor: a business searching "Treniko"
finds a Page rather than nothing, and the Page is the prerequisite for any
future advertising — which is **not** happening (€0, no ad account, no payment
method).

## What Facebook can do that Instagram cannot

Verified this session in the Planner:

| | Instagram | Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule from Business Suite | ❌ not until the accounts are linked | ✅ **works today** |
| Clickable links in post copy | ❌ | ✅ **yes** |
| Long-form copy that gets read | mostly no | yes |
| Link previews | ❌ | ✅ |

**The clickable link is the one that matters.** Instagram has exactly one
clickable link, in the bio. Every Facebook post can carry one. For a product
whose entire funnel is "get to treniko.com", that is not a small difference —
and it is the only argument for Facebook-native content that survives scrutiny.

## The split

### Cross-post from Instagram — the default

| Instagram format | Goes to Facebook? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Feed carousel | **yes** | same images, **rewritten caption** with the link |
| Feed single | **yes** | same |
| Reel | **yes** | same MP4 — Facebook Reels use the same 9:16, 15 s format |
| Story | **no** | 0 followers means 0 views. Revisit above ~100 followers |

**Rewrite the caption, do not paste it.** An Instagram caption ends in "link in
bio" and carries its hashtags in a first comment. Both are wrong on Facebook:
the link goes *in the post*, and Facebook hashtags do almost nothing. Pasting an
Instagram caption verbatim is the clearest possible signal that nobody is
actually running the Page.

Rewrite rule, mechanical enough to follow at 22:00:

1. Delete "link in bio" → replace with the tagged URL, on its own last line
2. Delete all hashtags except one or two
3. Keep the hook line **exactly** as it is — it was the most-worked sentence in
   the post and it works on any platform

### Facebook-native — rare, and only for one reason

Write native only when the post **needs the clickable link to work**. That is
roughly one post a fortnight, not a parallel content calendar.

Two examples worth doing:

- **"What TRENIKO actually is"** — a pinned Page post, long-form, with the link.
  The Facebook equivalent of PIN 1. Write it before the first cross-post so an
  arriving visitor lands on something that explains the product.
- **A real release note**, when there is one. Not a roadmap, not a teaser.

### Instagram-only — stays Instagram-only

- Polls and question boxes. Facebook Page Stories at 0 followers collect nothing,
  and the answers are the entire point of those Stories.
- Highlights. No Facebook equivalent worth the effort.
- Anything whose CTA is "send this to a trainer". That behaviour is an Instagram
  behaviour.

## Cadence

**Two to three posts a week, all cross-posts, plus one native post a fortnight.**

Do not post daily to a Page with 0 followers. It produces nothing except a
timeline that makes the account look busier than it is, and it is time not spent
on M1 and M2 in `MANUAL_QUEUE.md` — which are worth more than every Facebook
post in this document combined.

**Do not** post the backlog of P01–P20 to Facebook all at once to "fill" the
Page. A Page with 20 posts in one hour and 0 followers looks worse than an empty
one. Start with the pinned native post, then cross-post forward from whatever
publishes next.

## How to schedule (this works today)

1. `business.facebook.com/latest/planner?asset_id=1300314106493001`
2. Confirm the asset selector top-left reads **Treniko**
3. **Create post** → pick images or video → write copy → **Schedule**
4. Verify by reloading the Planner and reading the item back off the calendar

Once `MANUAL_QUEUE.md` § M1 is done, this same Planner will schedule Instagram
too, and the two calendars merge into one.

## Growing the Page — and the one thing not to do

At 0 followers the Page grows from the Instagram account, the website footer,
and search. Slowly. That is fine and it should not be forced.

> ❌ **Do not use "Invite friends to follow this Page."**
> Business Suite pushes this hard. It invites a *personal* network — friends and
> family, none of whom are personal trainers — to follow a product Page. It buys
> a follower number that is entirely noise, it permanently wrecks the Page's
> engagement rate as a signal, and it spams people who did not ask. It is the
> Facebook equivalent of buying followers, and it is on the *Never* list in
> `STRATEGY.md` for the same reason.

Also never: engagement-bait ("tag a trainer who needs this"), reposting other
people's content without permission, joining trainer Groups purely to drop
links, or any paid promotion.

## What would make this Page worth more effort

Reassess when **any** of these is true — not before:

- The Page passes ~100 organic followers
- Facebook referral traffic shows up in analytics (which requires
  `ANALYTICS_PLAN.md` to be implemented first — nothing measures this today)
- A trainer community on Facebook turns out to be a real acquisition channel
- TRENIKO decides to advertise, which is a separate decision with a separate
  budget conversation

Until then Facebook is a **cross-post destination and a scheduling tool**, and
the honest measure of success for this Page in the next 30 days is that it
unblocked Instagram scheduling — not its follower count.

Stories cannot be posted from Instagram web.** So every highlight below is
created in the **mobile app**, after the matching Stories in
`STORY_PUBLISH_QUEUE.md` have gone out.

**Do not create an empty or filler highlight.** A highlight with one weak Story
in it is worse than no highlight.

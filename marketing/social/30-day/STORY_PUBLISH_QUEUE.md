## How to post one (about 20 seconds each)

1. Instagram app -> **+** -> **Story** -> pick the PNG from your camera roll.
2. If the row below says **Poll** or **Question**, add the real interactive
   sticker on top and type the exact text given. The options drawn on the image
   are a **visual guide only** — without the real sticker nobody can answer, and
   the answers are the entire point.
3. Share to Story.

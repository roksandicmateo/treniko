# TRENIKO — Instagram metrics template

Fill in roughly 48 hours after each post publishes (Instagram Insights →
the post → View insights). Fill the same row again at day 7 for the slower
metrics — saves and shares keep accruing.

## Why these columns

Likes are the least useful number on this list. For a SaaS brand at zero
audience, the metrics that actually predict growth are **non-follower reach**
(did it travel?), **saves** (was it useful?), **shares** (would they hand it to
another trainer?) and **profile visits → website clicks** (did it convert?).

## Derived ratios worth watching

| Ratio | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Save rate | saves ÷ reach | the clearest signal that a post was genuinely useful |
| Share rate | shares ÷ reach | the only metric that compounds — shares are how a zero-audience account grows |
| Non-follower share of reach | non-follower reach ÷ reach | above ~50% means the format is being distributed beyond followers |
| Visit rate | profile visits ÷ reach | did the content make anyone curious about the brand |
| Click-through | website clicks ÷ profile visits | is the bio doing its job |

## Review checkpoints

**Day 7 (24 Aug) · Day 14 (31 Aug) · Day 21 (7 Sep) · Day 30 (16 Sep)**

At each checkpoint, answer only these:

1. Which **hook** got the highest non-follower reach?
2. Which post got the most **saves**? Most **shares**?
3. Which **format** (carousel / single / Reel) outperformed on non-follower reach?
4. Which **pillar** drove profile visits and website clicks?
5. What is the one thing to do more of in the next seven days?

Do not rewrite the calendar on a single post's performance. Two data points are
noise; the day-14 checkpoint is the first one worth acting on.

## Sample-size honesty

The account starts at 1 follower. Early numbers will be tiny and extremely noisy.
Do not compute percentages off a reach of 12 and treat them as findings, and do
not publish any of these numbers as marketing claims.

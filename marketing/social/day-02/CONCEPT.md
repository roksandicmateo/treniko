# TRENIKO — Instagram Day 02 — Concept (PREPARED, NOT PUBLISHED)

Theme: **problem awareness**. Day 1 stated the promise; Day 2 names the pain the
reader is living in right now, without yet selling the fix.

## Hook (slide 1 / primary headline)

> Your coaching business is spread across five apps.

Alternates, same register:
- "Where does your coaching business actually live?"
- "Five apps. One business."

## Format recommendation

**Carousel, 3 slides, 1080 × 1350.**

Rationale: the whole point is the *fragmentation* — WhatsApp, calendar, notes,
spreadsheet, payments. A single static image has to cram five things into one
frame and becomes the clutter it is criticising. A carousel lets the swipe itself
perform the argument: scattered → cost → consolidated. Three slides keeps the
completion rate high; five would not add information.

If a single image is preferred instead, use slide 2 alone — it is the strongest
standalone frame.

## Slide structure

**Slide 1 — the hook**
- Same layout system as Day 1: TRENIKO wordmark top-left, huge dark headline, `#0ea5e9` rule
- Headline: "Your coaching business is spread across five apps."
- No supporting copy. The headline carries it.

**Slide 2 — the scatter (the money frame)**
- Five loose, slightly rotated light-grey rounded cards on white, deliberately
  misaligned so the eye cannot settle — the clutter is the message
- Card labels, plain text only, no brand logos: `WhatsApp` · `Calendar` · `Notes` ·
  `Spreadsheet` · `Payments`
- Small line beneath: "Five places to look. One client to answer."
- No third-party logos or icons — avoids trademark use and keeps it Canva-Free-safe

**Slide 3 — the resolution**
- The same five labels, now stacked as one clean aligned column inside a single
  bordered card, `#0ea5e9` left edge — visually the opposite of slide 2
- Headline: "Or one place."
- Supporting: "Clients. Sessions. Payments. Progress."
- CTA: `treniko.com`

## Caption concept

Opening beat — name the reality, do not exaggerate:

> A client messages you on WhatsApp to move tomorrow's session.
> You check the calendar. You update the spreadsheet. You make a note about the
> package they have left. Four apps, one small change.

Middle beat — the cost, stated plainly and without invented numbers:

> None of it is hard. It is just constant. And it is the part of coaching nobody
> signed up for.

Close — the product, one line, no overclaiming:

> TRENIKO keeps clients, sessions, payments and progress in one place.
>
> → treniko.com

Hashtags: reuse the Day 1 set. Do not expand it.

## Guardrails carried over
- English only
- No invented statistics, testimonials or user counts
- No third-party brand logos in the artwork — text labels only
- No "AI-powered" framing
- Same type scale, margins and colour roles as Day 1 so the grid reads as one system

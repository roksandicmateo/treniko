## M1 — Connect Instagram to the Facebook Page ⚠️ DO THIS FIRST

**Why it is first:** it converts four other blocked things into solved things.
Once the link exists, Meta Business Suite can schedule Instagram feed posts,
**Reels and Stories** — which is the durable fix for the bottleneck that has
survived five sessions.

Do it in the **Instagram mobile app**, which is the path Meta actually supports;
the desktop flow dead-ends on a popup (`SOCIAL_AUDIT.md` § 4).

1. Instagram app → your profile → **☰** → **Settings and privacy**
2. **Accounts Centre** → **Connected experiences** → **Accounts**
3. **Add accounts** → **Add Facebook account** → sign in if asked
4. Choose the Page named **Treniko** — the one with the blue T avatar

> ⚠️ **Read the name before you tap.** The list also contains *FC Barcelona
> Fans*, *La Liga*, *Manchester United Balkan Fans*, *Lighters Hrvatska*,
> *Zdrava Navada* and *Zašto je Bog stvorio Real?*. Picking one of those would
> put TRENIKO Reels into a football fan page's publishing queue.

5. Instagram will offer to share Instagram DMs into the Page inbox. **Decline.**
   That was deliberately turned off during setup and there is no reason to
   reverse it yet.

**Then verify, don't assume:** open
`business.facebook.com/latest/home?asset_id=1300314106493001`. The Weekly plan
tile should read **Connect to Instagram 1/1**, and the header should show the
`treniko_fitness` handle. If it does not say `treniko_fitness`, something is
connected that should not be — disconnect it before posting anything.
